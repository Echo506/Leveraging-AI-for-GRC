# Lesson 12: Where Does AI Fit Legally?

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Identify legal and ethical concerns related to AI
- Explain why AI governance requires legal awareness
- Recognize privacy and intellectual property risks
- Describe appropriate actions when AI use is unclear

## Technology and the Law

Technology and the law are often in a race with each other.

New technologies can develop and spread faster than laws, regulations, and legal guidance. This can create uncertainty for organizations and individuals using AI.

As AI becomes more common, organizations must consider how it affects the rights and privacy of people.

## Key Legal Concerns

Legal and ethical concerns related to AI may include:

- Privacy violations
- Improper use of personal data
- Intellectual property violations
- Use of creative work without permission
- Lack of accountability for harmful AI use
- Violations of local laws or regulations
- Use of AI outside the appropriate jurisdiction

## Intellectual Property and Permission

AI tools may use, reproduce, transform, or generate content related to existing work.

Organizations should consider whether content, art, data, code, or other material is being used with appropriate permission.

They should also understand the ownership terms, licensing conditions, and intellectual property implications of AI-generated output.

## AI in Education

AI may be used inappropriately by students from elementary school through college.

Depending on the institution’s rules, this may violate an honor code and could be considered plagiarism or another form of academic misconduct.

Students should understand what AI use is allowed, what must be disclosed, and when original work is required.

## Accountability and Responsible Use

Organizations that create or use AI should be accountable for potential wrongdoing.

People with knowledge of both technology and law are needed to help create protections, clarify responsibilities, and hold organizations accountable.

## Free Does Not Mean Risk-Free

A free AI tool may still create costs or risks.

For example, an organization may expose confidential information, accept unfavorable terms, lose control over data, or violate legal or contractual obligations.

Before using an AI tool, users should review the applicable terms, privacy practices, data-handling rules, and organizational policies.

## Ask for Legal Guidance

If you are unsure whether an AI use case is appropriate in a professional or educational setting, ask your legal team or compliance team for clarification.

Use AI ethically, within appropriate boundaries, and in accordance with the laws and jurisdiction that apply to your situation.

## Key Takeaway

AI use creates legal, privacy, intellectual property, and accountability considerations. Organizations and individuals must use AI ethically, respect applicable laws and institutional policies, and seek legal guidance when they are uncertain.

## Review Questions

1. Why can the law struggle to keep up with AI technology?
2. Name two legal concerns related to AI.
3. Why is creator permission important when using AI?
4. How can AI use become academic misconduct?
5. What should you do if you are unsure whether an AI use case is legally appropriate?
