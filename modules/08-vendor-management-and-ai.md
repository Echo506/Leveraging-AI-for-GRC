# Lesson 8: Vendor Management and AI

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Explain why vendor management is important in GRC
- Identify common challenges in vendor security assessments
- Describe how AI can improve vendor questionnaires
- Recognize the need for human validation and business judgment

## What Is Vendor Management?

Vendor management is the process of evaluating, monitoring, and managing third-party organizations that provide products or services to a company.

Vendors may introduce risks related to:

- Cybersecurity
- Privacy
- Data protection
- Regulatory compliance
- Business continuity
- Operational reliability

Because vendors can affect an organization’s environment, their security information must be current, detailed, and reliable.

## Traditional Vendor Assessments

Organizations commonly send questionnaires to potential vendors.

These questionnaires may be standard forms or unique assessments created for a specific vendor. The vendor is asked to answer questions and provide documentation or evidence to support its claims.

This process can be repetitive, time-consuming, and difficult for both the requesting organization and the vendor.

## How AI Can Help

AI can help requesters create more focused questions based on:

- The type of vendor
- The service being provided
- The data involved
- The risk the organization wants to address
- Relevant security and compliance requirements

This can create shorter and more relevant questionnaires that focus on the organization’s real concerns.

## AI Support for Vendors

Vendors may use AI to help:

- Draft questionnaire responses
- Find relevant security information
- Summarize security posture
- Create evidence lists
- Prepare a security one-pager
- Document controls and practices

A security one-pager is a concise document that summarizes a vendor’s security posture, key controls, certifications, and important practices.

## Human Validation

AI can reduce repetitive work, but answers and evidence must still be reviewed.

People must verify:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Currency
- Relevance
- Alignment with contractual and regulatory requirements

The goal is not simply to complete hundreds of questions. The goal is to make informed decisions and build appropriate vendor relationships.

## Key Takeaway

AI can make vendor management more efficient by creating targeted questionnaires, supporting responses, and organizing evidence. Human reviewers remain responsible for verifying information, assessing risk, and making sound business decisions.
