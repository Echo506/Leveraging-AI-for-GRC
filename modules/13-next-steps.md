# Lesson 13: Next Steps

## Course Conclusion

AI has a large and evolving future in Governance, Risk, and Compliance.

Its impact on GRC and the security community will continue to grow. However, AI adoption should be thoughtful, responsible, and aligned with organizational requirements.

## Before Implementing AI

Before implementing an AI tool or AI-supported process, organizations should consider:

- The expected benefits
- Potential risks and drawbacks
- Governance requirements
- Legal and regulatory considerations
- Privacy and confidentiality
- Data classification and handling
- Human oversight
- The organization’s risk appetite
- Internal policies and approval processes

## Check Organizational Rules

Before using AI in a company, school, or university, users should understand the organization’s position on AI use cases.

They should verify:

- Whether the AI tool is approved
- What types of data may be entered into the tool
- Whether human review is required
- Which teams must approve high-risk AI use cases
- Whether legal, privacy, security, or compliance review is needed

## Responsible AI Mindset

AI should be used as a tool to support people, not as an automatic replacement for human accountability.

GRC and security professionals should continue to:

- Apply professional judgment
- Protect confidential data
- Validate AI-generated outputs
- Monitor risks and controls
- Follow legal and organizational requirements
- Communicate clearly with stakeholders

## Key Takeaway

AI creates valuable opportunities for GRC, but responsible adoption requires a clear understanding of its benefits, limitations, governance obligations, and legal implications.

## Final Reflection Questions

1. How could AI improve a GRC activity in your organization?
2. What data should never be entered into a public AI tool?
3. What risks should be assessed before adopting an AI solution?
4. Who should review and approve a high-risk AI use case?
5. How will you apply responsible AI practices in your future IT or cybersecurity role?
