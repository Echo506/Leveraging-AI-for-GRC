# Lesson 4: Big Data and AI Processing

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Define big data in a GRC context
- Explain how data supports risk identification
- Describe how AI can enhance big data processing
- Identify limitations and responsible-use considerations

## What Is Big Data?

Big data refers to large and complex datasets that come from different sources and require processing.

Organizations use data to identify:

- Patterns
- Facts
- Behaviors
- Historical trends
- Current conditions
- Potential future risks

## Big Data in GRC

Organizations with high volumes of data and transactions can use big data to strengthen GRC activities.

For example, a bank can analyze transaction data to understand customer activity, identify unusual behavior, assess possible fraud, and recognize risks that may be developing.

## How AI Uses Data

AI systems depend on data to learn, improve, and become more useful.

When an organization invests in big data for GRC, the data may be used by AI tools to enhance:

- Risk identification
- Data processing
- Notifications and alerts
- Risk management activities
- Trend analysis

In general, more detailed and relevant data can help AI identify meaningful patterns.

## Better-Informed Decisions

AI-supported analysis can help organizations make more informed decisions about how to handle risks.

This may reduce the organization’s overall risk footprint by enabling teams to identify issues earlier, prioritize risks, and respond more effectively.

## Important Considerations

More data does not automatically mean better decisions.

Organizations must consider:

- Data quality
- Data accuracy
- Privacy and confidentiality
- Bias in data
- Access controls
- Human review of AI outputs
- Legal and regulatory requirements

## Key Takeaway

Big data helps GRC teams understand risks, behavior, and patterns at scale. AI can enhance data processing, risk identification, alerts, and management capabilities, but organizations must use reliable data and maintain human oversight.
