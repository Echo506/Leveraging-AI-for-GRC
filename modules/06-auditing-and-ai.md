# Lesson 6: Auditing and AI

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Describe common auditing activities
- Explain how AI can support policy reviews
- Identify how AI may improve audit-evidence collection and review
- Recognize the importance of human judgment in auditing

## Auditing in GRC

Auditing is an important GRC practice used to assess whether an organization follows applicable policies, controls, frameworks, baselines, and regulations.

Traditional audit activities often include:

- Scheduling and attending meetings
- Requesting audit evidence
- Collecting and organizing evidence
- Reviewing policies
- Testing controls
- Reviewing populations and samples
- Identifying deviations
- Writing audit reports

Many of these activities are manual and time-consuming.

## AI for Policy Review

AI can help auditors read and review policies more efficiently.

For example, an AI tool may analyze a policy and highlight sections related to:

- Applicable regulations
- Security frameworks
- Organizational baselines
- Required controls
- Potential gaps or missing information

This can help auditors prepare stronger questions for follow-up conversations with process owners and auditees.

## AI for Audit Evidence

Audit evidence demonstrates whether a control was performed or whether a requirement was met.

AI can help auditees identify, organize, and gather the evidence required for an audit more quickly.

For auditors, AI can assist with reviewing:

- Data populations
- Sample sizes
- Evidence completeness
- Exceptions
- Deviations from expected controls

## Reducing Error and Bias

AI-supported review may make parts of the audit process less vulnerable to human error and potentially more independent from human bias.

However, AI output must be validated by qualified auditors. AI should support—not replace—professional judgment, auditor independence, and accountability.

## Benefits of AI in Auditing

When implemented responsibly, AI can help:

- Reduce repetitive manual work
- Speed up policy and evidence reviews
- Improve preparation for audit meetings
- Identify potential gaps and deviations
- Give auditors more time to write reports
- Improve communication with auditees and clients

## Key Takeaway

AI can streamline auditing by supporting policy review, evidence gathering, population and sample analysis, and deviation detection. Human auditors remain responsible for validating conclusions, conducting professional discussions, and issuing reliable audit reports.
