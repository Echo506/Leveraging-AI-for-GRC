# Lesson 10: Positives of AI in GRC

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Identify key benefits of AI in GRC
- Explain how AI can reduce repetitive work
- Describe how AI gives GRC professionals more time for strategic work
- Recognize the importance of human interaction in GRC

## AI Supports, Not Replaces, People

GRC is a profession that requires human interaction.

Relationship building, emotional intelligence, business knowledge, and communication are essential skills for GRC practitioners.

AI can reduce the time spent on repetitive tasks so professionals can focus on the activities that require human judgment and influence.

## Benefits of AI in GRC

AI can help organizations:

- Save time
- Streamline repetitive processes
- Analyze information faster
- Support risk identification
- Prepare documentation
- Improve audit preparation
- Simplify vendor questionnaire responses
- Create metrics and dashboards
- Track regulatory updates
- Identify changes between regulations and the organization’s environment

## More Time for Strategic Work

Instead of spending hours trying to identify and explain a risk, GRC professionals can use AI-supported analysis to spend more time creating a clear narrative for the business.

They can explain:

- Why a risk should be accepted
- Why a risk should be mitigated
- Which security initiative should be prioritized
- How a proposed change will improve the organization’s security posture

## Auditing Benefits

External auditors may spend less time collecting evidence manually.

This can allow them to focus more on:

- Walkthroughs
- Interviews
- Risk discussions
- Audit conclusions
- Client relationships

## Vendor Management Benefits

AI can help organizations prepare a vendor security dossier.

A vendor dossier may include security documentation, evidence, policies, controls, certifications, and common questionnaire responses.

This can reduce the need to repeatedly complete long vendor security questionnaires.

## Metrics and Regulatory Updates

AI can help create metrics and dashboards.

It may also help organizations understand changes between regulatory updates and their current environment before they make important changes.

## Positive Impact

AI can help GRC practitioners become more hands-on and effective in driving positive security outcomes.

It can support well-informed decisions for practitioners, stakeholders, and business leaders.

## Key Takeaway

AI can automate and streamline repetitive GRC work. This gives professionals more time to communicate risk, build relationships, influence stakeholders, improve security initiatives, and make informed decisions.

## Review Questions

2. Name three GRC activities that AI can streamline.

4. What is a vendor security dossier?
5. How can AI help with regulatory updates?
