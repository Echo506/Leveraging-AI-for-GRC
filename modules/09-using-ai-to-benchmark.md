# Lesson 9: Using AI to Benchmark

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Define benchmarking in a GRC and cybersecurity context
- Identify security frameworks used as benchmarking references
- Explain how AI can support control-gap analysis
- Describe why human review is necessary before making changes

## What Is Benchmarking?

Benchmarking is the act of assessing something against a point of reference.

In cybersecurity, organizations use benchmarking to compare their controls, policies, and practices against recognized standards and frameworks.

Benchmarking helps answer questions such as:

- How effective are our security controls?
- Which controls are missing or incomplete?
- Where do we need to improve?
- Which risks should we prioritize?

## Security Frameworks

Common frameworks and standards used for benchmarking include:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF)
- Center for Internet Security Controls (CIS Controls)
- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27002

The appropriate framework depends on the organization’s industry, size, regulatory environment, customers, and security-program maturity.

## Why Benchmarking Matters

Benchmarking can help an organization understand its current security posture without immediately requiring a formal audit.

It can reveal:

- Control gaps
- Weak or missing processes
- Areas of elevated risk
- Opportunities for policy improvement
- Priorities for a security roadmap

## How AI Can Help

Some security and compliance platforms already provide benchmarking capabilities.

AI can enhance benchmarking by helping organizations:

- Analyze gaps against a selected framework
- Review large volumes of control evidence
- Identify risks related to missing controls
- Recommend potential remediation actions
- Adapt recommendations to the organization’s environment
- Track progress over time

## Human Review Before Changes

AI recommendations should not be implemented automatically.

Changes to systems, controls, or configurations can create unintended consequences and may affect customers, operations, security, or compliance.

Human stakeholders must review whether a recommendation:

- Is technically safe
- Fits the business environment
- Aligns with risk appetite
- Meets regulatory obligations
- Will not negatively affect customers or operations

## Key Takeaway

AI can strengthen benchmarking by identifying control gaps and suggesting improvements based on the organization’s environment. However, people must validate recommendations before making changes because security improvements can create operational or customer impact.
